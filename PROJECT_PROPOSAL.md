# Land ReGen: SDG 15 Project Proposal

## Executive Summary

Land ReGen is a comprehensive digital platform addressing UN Sustainable Development Goal 15 (Life on Land) by connecting communities, providing education, and facilitating land restoration projects worldwide. The platform combines project management, educational resources, community engagement, and AI-powered guidance to accelerate terrestrial ecosystem restoration.

## Problem Statement

### Global Challenge

- **3.2 billion people** are affected by land degradation globally
- **24 billion tons** of fertile soil are lost annually
- **1 million species** face extinction due to habitat loss
- **Land degradation** contributes to 23% of global greenhouse gas emissions
- **Desertification** threatens food security for millions

### Local Impact

Land degradation affects:
- Agricultural productivity
- Water availability
- Biodiversity
- Climate resilience
- Community livelihoods

## Risk Assessment
